Style guide v1 for the new Medcare website, locked on 3 October 2026. Colours, type, buttons, glass cards, service badge cards, step tracker, doctor card and the mobile action bar, using real Medcare content. Hover, click and swipe to try them.

Build notes for Windsurf · read before building
  • Locked Fonts: Figtree (all UI and text), Instrument Serif italic (one accent word per heading, at most), Noto Sans Devanagari (only if Hindi or Bodo text appears). Load with next/font, self-hosted, preload only Figtree.
  • Locked Colours: copy the :root tokens in this file exactly. Teal comes from the Medcare logo; the deep slate and the two-part Book button follow Oliva's pattern. Do not change any value. --aqua and --accent are decorative only and never used for text.
  • One gradient surface per viewport: primary buttons, the active tab, the Book button and the mobile bar. Everything else is white with tints.
  • Hero: Dr. Das is the only doctor, so the hero is ONE large portrait card. No fan, no swap.
  • Logo: the supplied logo is a PNG. Redraw it faithfully as SVG (full, white and heart-mark versions) and submit it for approval before use. The PNGs in resources/images/brand/ are for reference and fallback.
  • Service cards: NO arch, box or card behind the badge. Each service is its illustrated PNG badge floating on the section wash with a soft round glow, then the title with a small gradient arrow and one line. Hover: orbit ring spins once, badge lifts, coin flip, sheen, arrow slides (section 03). The badges here are placeholders that show the style and colours.
  • Mobile: any section with more than three cards becomes a self-moving swipe carousel below 768px (every 4.5s, pause on touch or hover, pause button, still under reduced motion).

Do

  • White page background everywhere; tints only for chips, tabs, washes and glows
  • One gradient surface per viewport
  • Teal text #007680 for links and eyebrows, so they pass on tints too
  • Peach and aqua only inside glows, blobs and badge highlights
  • Red only for "Medical emergency? Call 108 or 112" and the emergency callout

Do not

  • Pure black text, or grey text lighter than #4A6570
  • Teal #00838F for small text on tints (fails 4.5:1)
  • Green, orange or other colours not in this palette (WhatsApp green only on the WhatsApp button)
  • A red cross or red plus anywhere
  • Dark full-width hero panels (Oliva's dark hero is not used; ours is white with glows)
