H1: Medical *disclaimer*

The information on this website is written and reviewed by Dr. Tamoghna Das, MBBS, MD (Medicine), to help patients understand common conditions, tests and care at Medcare. It is general information only.

- It is not a diagnosis or treatment plan for you. Every person is different, and the right advice depends on your history, examination and tests.
- Do not start, stop or change any medicine based on this website. Talk to your doctor first.
- Do not delay seeking medical care because of something you read here.
- Tests and treatments mentioned may not be suitable for everyone. Dr. Das decides which tests and treatments you need after examining you.
- Results of care differ from person to person. We do not promise any particular outcome.
- Numbers such as target ranges are general guides from published guidelines. Your own targets may differ.

**Emergency:** if you think you or someone else has a medical emergency, call 108 or 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency now. Do not use the website form or WhatsApp for emergencies.

**Mental health:** if you are in distress or having thoughts of harming yourself, call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (free, 24 hours) or 112.
