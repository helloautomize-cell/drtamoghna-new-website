H1: Patient rights and *responsibilities*

The Charter of Patients' Rights is displayed in our waiting area. You can also read it on the website of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare. [Link: official Charter of Patients' Rights, MoHFW / NHRC]

### Your rights at Medcare
1. **Information.** To know your diagnosis, the reason for tests, the treatment options, their benefits, risks and likely costs, in a language you understand.
2. **Records.** To receive copies of your reports and medical records, as provided under medical regulations.
3. **Consent.** To agree or refuse tests and treatment after a clear explanation. Written consent is taken for procedures that need it.
4. **Privacy and confidentiality.** To be examined with privacy, with a chaperone if you wish, and to have your information kept confidential.
5. **Second opinion.** To seek another doctor's opinion. We will share your records for this.
6. **Transparent charges.** To know the charges for consultations and tests before they are done, and to receive an itemised bill.
7. **Choice.** To choose where to buy medicines and where to have lab tests or hospital care. You are never required to use a particular pharmacy, lab or hospital.
8. **Non-discrimination.** To receive care without discrimination based on illness, religion, caste, gender, age, language, sexual orientation, disability or place of origin.
9. **Safety and quality.** To receive care that follows accepted medical standards in a clean, safe clinic.
10. **Be heard.** To give feedback or make a complaint and receive a fair response.

### Your responsibilities
- Give complete and honest information about your health, medicines and allergies
- Follow the treatment plan you agreed to, or tell us if you cannot
- Come on time, or tell us early if you need to change your appointment
- Treat staff and other patients with respect
- Pay charges for services received
- Do not share other patients' information you may see or hear at the clinic
