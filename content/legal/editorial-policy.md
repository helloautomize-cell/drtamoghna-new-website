H1: Editorial and medical review *policy*

### Who writes and reviews our content
Every medical page and article on this website is reviewed and approved by **Dr. Tamoghna Das**, MBBS, MD (Medicine), registered with the Assam Council of Medical Registration (No. 20276). His name and the date of the last review appear at the end of each page.

### How we write
- Plain language for patients and families
- Answers first, details after
- Medical terms explained when first used
- Indian guidelines and Indian data where they exist; respected international guidelines where they do not
- No promises of results, no comparisons with other doctors or clinics, no testimonials, no medicine brand names

### Sources
We rely on guidelines and publications from bodies such as the Indian Council of Medical Research, the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, the Association of Physicians of India, RSSDI, the World Health Organization, and international specialty societies. Each page lists its main sources.

### Reviews and updates
- Every medical page is reviewed at least once a year
- Pages are updated sooner when guidelines change or an error is found
- The "Last reviewed" date shows the latest review

### Use of technology and AI tools
Some first drafts of text, and some illustrations such as service badges, are prepared with the help of digital and AI tools. Every word is checked, edited and approved by Dr. Das before publication. AI-assisted images are used only for illustrations and design, never to show real patients or results. Any edited or enhanced photo of Dr. Das is used only with his written approval.

### Photos of people
People shown in photos at the clinic have given written consent. Photos never show patient results, and captions never describe anyone's diagnosis.

### Independence
Medcare does not accept payment or sponsorship for content. We do not promote medicines, devices or brands. If a conflict of interest ever arises, it will be stated on the page.

### Report an error
If you think something on this website is wrong or out of date, please email medcarebongaigaon@gmail.com or call +91 60031 21297. We aim to review reports within 4 days.
