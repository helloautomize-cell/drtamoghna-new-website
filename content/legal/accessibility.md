H1: Accessibility *statement*

We want everyone to be able to use this website and visit our clinic, including older adults and people with disabilities.

### The website
This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We have:
- used large, readable text (at least 17px) with strong colour contrast
- added an A / A+ button to make text larger
- made every page usable with a keyboard and a screen reader
- given images text descriptions
- made buttons and links large enough to tap easily
- reduced or stopped motion for people who prefer less movement
- kept video and maps off until you choose to load them
- written in plain language

Some third-party content, such as embedded maps, may not be fully accessible. We provide the address and directions as text too.
