H1: Privacy *policy*

### 1. who we are
{IDENTITY}
For this policy, Dr. Tamoghna Das, as owner of Medcare, decides why and how your personal data is used. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), this makes Medcare the "data fiduciary".

### 2. what this policy covers
This policy explains how we handle personal data you share with us through this website, by phone, WhatsApp or email, and at the clinic. It applies to patients, family members and visitors to this website.

### 3. the information we collect

**Through this website**
- Booking form: your name, mobile number, the type of visit, the reason or test you pick, your chosen day and time slot, and a note (optional)
- Contact by email or WhatsApp: the details you choose to send
- Technical data: basic device and browser data needed to run and secure the website. With your consent only, anonymous usage data through Google Analytics (see section 9)

When you request an appointment online, your name, mobile number, visit reason, chosen time and note are saved to a private Google Sheet that only Medcare staff can open. We use them only to arrange your visit.

We ask you not to send detailed medical information through the website form.

**At the clinic**
- Identity and contact details
- Medical history, symptoms, examination findings, test results, prescriptions and reports
- Payment records (we do not store full card details)
- Photos or videos only with separate written consent

### 4. why we use your information
- To arrange, confirm and change appointments
- To provide medical care, tests and follow-up
- To keep medical records as required by law
- To contact you about your care and reports
- To answer your questions and complaints
- To keep the website working and secure
- To understand, in anonymous form, how the website is used (only with your consent)

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with drug companies.

### 5. legal basis
We use your personal data:
- with your consent, which you give when you submit the booking form or share information with us; and
- for legitimate uses allowed under the DPDP Act, such as providing medical treatment, responding to a medical emergency, and meeting legal duties such as keeping medical records.

You can withdraw consent at any time (section 10). Withdrawal does not affect care already provided or records we must keep by law.

### 6. children
For patients under 18, we ask that a parent or legal guardian make the appointment request and give consent at the clinic. We do not knowingly collect children's data for any purpose other than their care, and we do not track or profile children.

### 7. who we share information with
Only as needed:
- **Clinic staff** involved in your care, who are bound to keep it confidential
- **Other doctors, labs or hospitals** you are referred to or choose, with your knowledge
- **Service providers** that help us run the clinic and website: messaging and email (WhatsApp, email), secure cloud storage, website hosting, Google (the private Google Sheet and Apps Script that store and alert us about booking requests), and, with consent, website analytics. Device makers' secure portals may process test data from monitoring devices.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it (for example, notifiable diseases or a court order)

Some of these providers may store data outside India, under their own security and privacy terms and in line with Indian law.

### 8. how long we keep information
- Medical records: at least 3 years from your last visit, or longer if needed for a legal case or complaint
- Website booking requests that do not lead to a visit: 12 months, then deleted
- Analytics data (only with consent): 2 months
After these periods, we delete or securely destroy the data.

### 9. cookies and analytics
The website uses essential cookies that are needed for it to work, such as remembering your cookie choice. With your consent only, we use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. No health information or form details are sent to analytics. You can change your choice at any time through "Cookie settings" in the footer.

### 10. your rights
Under the DPDP Act you can:
- ask what personal data we hold about you and how it is used
- ask us to correct or update inaccurate data
- ask us to delete data we no longer need to keep by law
- withdraw your consent
- nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to
- raise a complaint with us, and if not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India

To use any of these rights, contact our privacy contact (section 12). We may need to confirm your identity first. Copies of medical records are provided as required under medical regulations.

### 11. how we protect information
- Paper records are kept in locked storage at the clinic
- Digital records are kept in password-protected accounts with two-step verification
- Only people who need the data for your care can access it
- The website uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Booking requests are stored in a private Google Sheet that only Medcare staff can open, not in a public database
If a data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
