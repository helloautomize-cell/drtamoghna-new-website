H1: Prediabetes Diet: What to Eat and What to Limit

**Quick answer**
A prediabetes diet focuses on vegetables, protein, high-fibre foods and whole grains, with controlled portions of rice, wheat and sweets, and fewer sugary drinks and refined snacks. Along with exercise and gentle weight loss, these changes can help bring blood sugar back toward normal.

### What to Eat More Of

- **Vegetables:** fill half your plate with non-starchy vegetables such as leafy greens, beans, gourd and cabbage.
- **Protein:** dal, legumes, eggs, fish, chicken, paneer and curd in sensible portions.
- **Whole grains and millets:** brown rice, oats, ragi, jowar, bajra and whole wheat in controlled portions.
- **Healthy fats:** nuts, seeds and cooking oils used in small amounts.
- **Fruit:** whole fruits in moderation rather than juices.

### What to Limit

- Sugary drinks, sweets and desserts.
- White rice, white bread, maida products and bakery items in large portions.
- Deep-fried snacks and packaged foods.
- Fruit juices and sweetened tea or coffee.
- Late-night heavy meals.

### Building a Balanced Plate

- Half the plate: vegetables.
- A quarter: protein.
- A quarter: carbohydrate, preferably whole grain.
- Eat slowly and stop when comfortably full.

### Helpful Habits

- Do not skip meals, especially breakfast.
- Eat vegetables or protein first, then carbohydrates.
- Take a 10 to 15 minute walk after meals.
- Drink water instead of sweet beverages.
- Read labels for added sugar.
- Sleep well, as poor sleep affects blood sugar.

### Weight and Activity

Losing a modest amount of weight and getting regular physical activity help the body use insulin better. A common goal is at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, as advised by your doctor.

### Key Takeaways

- Fill half the plate with vegetables and choose whole grains.
- Limit sugary drinks, sweets and refined foods.
- Walk after meals and stay active.
- Regular follow-up tests track your progress.

### FAQs

1. **Can I eat rice with prediabetes?**
   Yes, in controlled portions with plenty of vegetables and protein.
2. **Are fruits allowed?**
   Yes, whole fruits in moderation are fine. Avoid juices.
3. **Are millets better than rice?**
   They have more fibre, but portion size still matters.
4. **Do I need to avoid sugar completely?**
   Limit it rather than fully avoid. Reduce sweets and sugary drinks.
5. **Can diet alone reverse prediabetes?**
   Diet, activity and weight loss together can help many people return to normal ranges.
6. **How often should I get tested?**
   Your doctor will advise, usually every six to twelve months.
