### Tests done at Medcare

| Test | What it shows | Time taken |
|---|---|---|
| ECG | Heart rhythm and signs of strain or past damage | About 5 minutes |
| Echocardiography with strain imaging | Heart structure, pumping, valves and early muscle changes | About 20 to 40 minutes |
| Patch Holter | Heart rhythm over 3 days or longer, at home | Fitted in about 10 minutes |
| 24-hour BP monitoring | Your blood pressure through a normal day and night | Fitted in about 10 minutes |
| HbA1c | Average sugar over about 3 months | Result during the visit |
| Finger-prick sugar | Sugar at that moment | A few minutes |
| Continuous glucose monitoring | Sugar every few minutes for up to 2 weeks | Sensor fitted in about 10 minutes |
| Spirometry | How well air moves in and out of the lungs | About 15 to 20 minutes |
| Body composition | Fat, muscle and water in the body | A few minutes |
| Diabetic foot check | Nerve feeling and blood flow in the feet | About 10 to 15 minutes |
| Sleep study | Breathing and oxygen during sleep | One night |

Note under table: Times are typical and can vary. Dr. Das decides which tests you need after examining you. [CONFIRM: typical times OK]
