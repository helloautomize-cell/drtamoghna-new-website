Intro: Short answers to the questions we hear most. For anything about your own health, please book a consultation.

### Booking and timings
1. **How do I book an appointment?**
   Use the booking form on our Contact page, call or WhatsApp [60031 21297](tel:+916003121297) or [93651 75235](tel:+919365175235). We confirm your time within 30 to 60 minutes during clinic hours. Requests sent after the clinic closes are confirmed the next morning.
2. **What are Medcare's timings?**
   Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 8:30 pm. Sunday, 9 am to 3:30 pm. Dr. Das's consultations are Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 2 pm and 6 pm to 8 pm, by prior appointment. On festival holidays the hours may change, so please call before you come.
3. **Can I walk in without booking?**
   Yes, but booked patients are seen at their reserved time, so you may need to wait for a free slot. Booking is the better option, especially if you need tests.
4. **What if I cannot come at my booked time?**
   Please call or WhatsApp us as early as you can so we can give the slot to someone else and find you a new time. There is no charge for changing or cancelling.

### Tests and reports
5. **Which tests are done at Medcare?**
   These tests are available at Medcare:
   1. 2D echocardiography with colour Doppler, tissue Doppler and strain imaging (cardiac speckle tracking), for adults and children: heart failure, valvular heart disease, prosthetic valves, congenital heart disease, cardiomyopathies, diabetic and hypertensive heart disease and more.
   2. ECG.
   3. Spirometry (lung function test).
   4. All blood, urine, stool and sputum tests.
   5. Fibroscan of the liver, on the 2nd Tuesday of every month.
   6. Ambulatory patch Holter for 72 hours, 7 days or up to 1 month of cardiac monitoring.
   7. CGM device for real-time, 14-day continuous blood sugar monitoring.
6. **Do I need to come fasting?**
   Only for some blood tests. Echo, ECG, Holter and most clinic tests need no fasting. When you book, tell us what you are coming for and we will tell you whether to fast. Do not skip diabetes medicines without asking.
7. **When will I get my echo report?**
   Dr. Das performs the echo himself and explains the findings straight after the scan. The written report is usually given the same day. [CONFIRM]
8. **Can I get a test without a consultation?**
   Dr. Das prefers to see you first, because the right test depends on your symptoms and history. If another doctor has referred you for an echo or Holter, bring the referral note and call us to arrange it. [CONFIRM: referral-only tests accepted?]

### Payment
9. **What payment methods do you accept?**
   Cash, UPI, and debit or credit cards.
10. **Do you accept health insurance or cashless?**
    Not for clinic visits. We give an itemised bill so you can claim from your insurer if your policy covers outpatient care. If you need admission, Dr. Das explains the hospital options and you choose one that suits your needs and insurance.
11. **Are fees shown on the website?**
    No. Charges depend on the consultation and tests you need. Please call 60031 21297 for current charges.

### Care and follow-up
12. **Do you sell medicines?**
    Yes. Medcare has its own pharmacy that sells genuine medicines, so you can buy prescribed medicines at the clinic with a proper bill.
13. **Can I ask a question on WhatsApp between visits?**
    You can send reports and simple questions on WhatsApp to 60031 21297 or 93651 75235. Treatment changes usually need a visit. WhatsApp is not watched for emergencies, so in an emergency call 108 or 112.
14. **Do you see children?**
    Medcare looks after adults and older teenagers, including young people with type 1 diabetes. Patients under 18 must come with a parent or guardian. [CONFIRM: minimum age]
15. **Do you offer online or video consultations?**
    Not at present. A first visit needs an examination in person. [CONFIRM]

### Privacy
16. **Who sees my health information?**
    Only Dr. Das and the clinic staff who need it for your care. We never sell your data. Read our Privacy Policy for details, including how to see, correct or delete your information.
