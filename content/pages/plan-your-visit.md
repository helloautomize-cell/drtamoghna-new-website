### What to bring

- All the medicines you take now, or their strips or a clear photo
- Old prescriptions and discharge summaries
- Test reports from the last 12 months (blood tests, ECG, echo, scans)
- Your sugar meter or diary, if you check sugar at home
- Your home BP readings, if you check BP at home
- For pregnancy-related visits: your antenatal card and scan reports
- A family member, if you would like help remembering the advice

### Fasting and test preparation

- **Echo, ECG, Holter, spirometry, body composition:** no fasting needed.
- **Fasting blood tests:** usually 8 to 10 hours without food. Water is fine. Ask when you book.
- **Spirometry:** avoid a heavy meal and smoking for a few hours before. Ask whether to skip your inhaler that morning.
- **Diabetes medicines:** do not stop or skip them on your own before a visit. If you are told to come fasting and you take insulin or sugar tablets, ask us how to adjust that morning's dose.

### Payment

We accept cash, UPI and debit or credit cards. Consultation and test charges are explained at the clinic or on the phone: call 60031 21297. We do not offer cashless or insurance billing for clinic visits, but we give an itemised bill so you can claim from your insurer if your policy covers outpatient care.

### Access

- Ground floor
- Wheelchair available at the clinic
- If you need help getting in, call when you arrive and a staff member will come out.

### Children and young people

Medcare looks after adults and older teenagers. Young people with type 1 diabetes are welcome. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian with them.


### FAQs

1. **How long does a first visit take?**
   Plan for about 45 minutes to 1 hour for a first visit, and longer if you need an echo or several tests. Follow-up visits are usually shorter. Booking ahead reduces waiting.
2. **Can a family member come into the consultation?**
   Yes. Many patients bring a family member to help remember the advice or to translate. Dr. Das will ask your permission first if they need to step out at any point.
3. **Do you give a written plan?**
   Yes. You receive a prescription with medicines, doses and timings, and the tests and follow-up date. Dr. Das also explains food, activity and warning signs before you leave.
4. **Can I send my reports on WhatsApp before the visit?**
   You can send reports on WhatsApp to 60031 21297 or 93651 75235 so the clinic has them ready. Please still bring the originals. WhatsApp is not monitored for emergencies.
